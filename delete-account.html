Account & Data Deletion

Request permanent deletion of your RepSense account and all associated fitness records.

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy and global data protection standards (including GDPR and CCPA), RepSense empowers you to permanently delete your account and all associated personal data at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the RepSense app installed on your Android device, you can delete your data immediately:

  1. Open the RepSense app.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (bottom navigation).
  3. Scroll down to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm deletion. All your local workouts, body metrics, steps history, and profile records will be wiped immediately.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request (Without App Installed)

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, submit a data deletion request directly to our team via email:

Email: bhavinmodhh@gmail.com
Subject: RepSense Account and Data Deletion Request
Information to provide: The email address linked with your Google Sign-In or your device account ID.

Send Deletion Request Email

Web deletion requests are verified and permanently fulfilled within 48 hours.

What Data Is Deleted?

Data Category Action Upon Deletion Retention Period
User Profile & Google ID Permanently purged from local preferences and records. 0 days (Immediate)
Workout History & Rep Logs Local SQLite database (repsense_sessions.db) wiped completely. 0 days (Immediate)
Body Metrics (Height, Weight, BMI) Permanently deleted. 0 days (Immediate)
Daily Steps & Calorie Records Purged from internal device logs. 0 days (Immediate)
Camera / Video Data Not applicable (Zero camera frames were ever saved or transmitted). Never stored

Active Google Play Subscriptions Note

Deleting your RepSense account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. To prevent unwanted charges from Google, cancel your subscription in the Google Play Subscriptions Center prior to deleting your account.
